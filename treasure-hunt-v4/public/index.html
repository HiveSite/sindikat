The evening they missed each other

After collecting all ten fragments, every team receives the same final clue and comes to the same concluding point.

Marko came on Friday the 18th. Ana came on Saturday the 19th. Both waited at the same place on different evenings. Both left believing the other had chosen not to come.

The date in the message had been altered. Neither abandoned the other. Their story ended because each carried a different version of the same promise.
